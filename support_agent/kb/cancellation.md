# Cancellation and Suspension

**Voluntary cancellation.** A customer may cancel at any time; service runs through the end of the current billing cycle and is not prorated on cancellation. There is no early-termination fee on postpaid plans. The agent should confirm the customer's intent and, if they proceed, escalate to a retention specialist to process the cancellation — the agent does not close accounts directly.

**Suspension for non-payment.** See billing policy; restored on payment.

**Voluntary suspension (travel/lost phone).** Lines can be suspended for up to 90 days. Billing pauses during a voluntary suspension. Processing a suspension is a human-agent action; escalate with the requested dates.

**Retention.** If a customer signals they want to leave (churn), surface relevant plan options that might fit better, but do not invent discounts or promotions. Discounts are offered only by retention specialists.
