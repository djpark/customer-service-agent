# Billing Policy

Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month and are due on the date shown on the invoice (typically the 1st of the following month). Payment methods: credit/debit card on file, or one-time card payment.

**Late payment.** If a balance is unpaid 15 days past the due date, the line may be suspended. A suspended line can be restored immediately once the past-due balance is paid; no reactivation fee applies.

**Disputes.** If a customer disputes a charge, do not promise a refund or credit. Acknowledge the dispute and escalate to a human billing specialist with a summary. Only billing specialists can issue credits.

**Overage.** Basic and Plus plans do not bill overage; data is throttled after the allowance instead. Premium Unlimited has no overage.

**Autopay.** Customers on autopay are charged on the due date using the card on file. We never read back a full card number — only the last four digits.
