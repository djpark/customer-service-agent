# Coverage and Outages

Coverage depends on the customer's area. To check for a known outage, look up the account and check line status, which reports any active outage in the account's ZIP area.

**During a known outage**, advise the customer that engineers are aware and share the ETA if one is available. Do not troubleshoot the device — the issue is network-side. Do not promise compensation; credits for outages, if any, are applied automatically and are handled by billing.

**No known outage but no service**: proceed to device troubleshooting (see troubleshooting guide). Common causes are a suspended line (check line status), airplane mode, or a SIM seated incorrectly.

We do not provide exact tower locations or network engineering details to customers.
