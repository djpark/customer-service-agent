# Plans and Pricing

We offer three postpaid plans. Prices are per line, per month, before taxes.

- **Basic 5GB** — $25/mo. 5 GB of high-speed data, then throttled to 128 kbps. No mobile hotspot.
- **Plus 25GB** — $45/mo. 25 GB of high-speed data, then throttled. Mobile hotspot included (uses plan data).
- **Premium Unlimited** — $70/mo. Unlimited high-speed data (may be deprioritized above 50 GB during congestion). Mobile hotspot included with 30 GB at high speed.

Plan changes take effect immediately and are prorated on the next invoice. A customer may change plans at any time; there is no fee to upgrade or downgrade. Downgrades that reduce data allowance below current-cycle usage do not retroactively add overage charges.
